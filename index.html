04 / Unfiled patents

Small annoyances,
slightly unreasonable fixes.

Things that bug me on an ordinary Tuesday, and what I'd do about them if anyone gave me a workshop and a free afternoon. None filed. All open to licensing for one (1) cutting chai.

No. 001status: unfiled

The honest horn

The annoyance
Indian traffic runs on horns. Most of them are pressed out of habit, not need, because the person pressing never has to hear it at full volume.
The fix
Wire the horn so it's exactly as loud inside the car as outside. Honk all you want. You'll just be honking at yourself.
Side effect
Very quiet cars. Slightly smug drivers.
No. 002status: unfiled

The whistle counter

The annoyance
"Switch it off after three whistles." Then someone talks to you, and was that two or four?
The fix
A small counter on the pressure cooker lid that ticks up with every whistle. Optional setting: it whistles a different tune on the last one.
Side effect
One less argument about the dal.
No. 003status: unfiled

The bucket that whistles

The annoyance
The water comes for an hour, you fill buckets, you get distracted, the bathroom floods.
The fix
A floating cap that rises with the water and whistles like a kettle when the bucket is nearly full.
Side effect
Morning water-time becomes a small orchestra.
No. 004status: unfiled

A fuel gauge for the inverter

The annoyance
Power cut. Is the inverter good for twenty minutes or four hours? Nobody knows until the fan slows down.
The fix
A simple dial in the living room, like a car's fuel gauge, that shows time left at the current load. Turn off the AC and watch it jump.
Side effect
The whole family learns which appliance is the villain.
No. 005status: unfiled

The polite double-park

The annoyance
Someone's parked behind you. Their number isn't on the dashboard. You're now late and honking at an empty car.
The fix
A windshield QR sticker. Scan it, tap "you're blocking me", and the owner's phone gets a nudge. Nobody's number is ever shared.
Side effect
Works brilliantly with No. 001.
No. 006status: unfiled

The geyser that texts back

The annoyance
Winter morning. You switch on the geyser, then guess. Too early and you're in a cold shower; too late and you're late.
The fix
A tiny light next to the geyser switch, outside the bathroom, that turns green when the water's actually hot.
Side effect
Ten more minutes under the blanket, guilt-free.
